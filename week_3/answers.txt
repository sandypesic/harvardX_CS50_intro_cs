Selection sort:
    Runtime = O(n^2) in all cases.
    Already-sorted input takes just as long as random input.
    Always scans the entire unsorted portion.

Bubble sort:
    Runtime = O(n^2) in worst case but O(n) in best case.
    Already-sorted input runs much faster than random or reversed input.
    Swaps adjacent elements.

Merge sort:
    Runtime = O(n log n) in all cases.
    Runs much faster than the other two algorithms.
    Recursively divides and conquers.
